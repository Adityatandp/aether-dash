# India sourcing notes (draft)

## Prototype (Phase 1–2)
- DevKits / displays: Robu.in, ElectronicsComp, Local maker stores, LCSC via JLCPCB
- PCBA: JLCPCB or local SMT in Bengaluru/Pune/Delhi NCR for small runs
- Enclosures: local FDM first; evaluate resin for face panels

## Production (Phase 4)
- Negotiate panel supply for consistent controller IC (avoid mixed ST7796/ILI9488 lots)
- Keep USB-C + ESD parts on JLCPCB basic parts list when possible
- Dual-source LDO and connector if possible

## Cost control
- Do not overspec aluminum CNC for v1
- Prefer 2-layer PCB until signal integrity proves otherwise
- Measure backlight current early — biggest wattage lever
